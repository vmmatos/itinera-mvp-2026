Lesson 0001 · Multi-Agent Negotiation Patterns

Negotiation Protocols 101: Announce / Bid / Award, and Where itinera Fits

Every negotiation protocol answers one question: who is allowed to say what, and when? Not what any agent actually proposes — that's strategy, and it's the agent's own business. The protocol is the fixed skeleton around it, enforced by the platform.

The oldest, simplest one for task allocation is the Contract Net Protocol (Reid G. Smith, 1980, later a FIPA standard). Three stages:

StageWhat happens
AnnounceA manager broadcasts a task, calling for proposals
BidContractors independently compute and submit offers
AwardManager evaluates all bids, picks one (or rejects and re-announces)

Primary source for this lesson: Wikipedia — Contract Net Protocol. Short, accurate, freely available — read it once before the quiz below. Full annotated list in RESOURCES.md.

Mapping it onto itinera

itinera's pipeline (from CLAUDE.md) is a fixed sequence of WorkflowStates, run by WorkflowEngine + WorkflowStateMachine:

LOGISTICS_PLANNING → FINANCE_ENVELOPE_CHECK → PARALLEL_TURISTIC_PLANNING
  → MERGE_AND_SCORE → FINANCE_VALIDATE
  → (ITINERARY_REPAIR ⇄ FINANCE_VALIDATE on budget rejects)
  → FINAL_VALIDATION → FINISH
itinera stateCNP equivalent
PARALLEL_TURISTIC_PLANNINGAnnounce + Bid — 4 preference agents (alice/bob/carlos/diana) each submit a proposal in parallel. No back-and-forth yet.
MERGE_AND_SCOREAward — orchestrator-agent acts as manager, picks/merges among the bids.
FINANCE_VALIDATEA second, budget-scoped bidder (finance-agent) with a soft veto — see glossary.
ITINERARY_REPAIRNot classical CNP. Instead of re-announcing (throwing out the award and re-bidding from scratch), it amends the existing award with a targeted edit list. Cheaper (~8k vs ~170k tokens/cycle) and closer to a contract amendment than a re-negotiation.

That last row is the interesting one — it's the part of itinera's design that classical CNP doesn't have an answer for, and it's exactly the pattern #42 needs to generalize into a real iterative loop.
